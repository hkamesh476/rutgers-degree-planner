Planning notes

Where the three programs affect each other.

Recommended

Take 640:151 for Calculus I

Economics accepts 130, 135 or 151, but the math minor names 151, and Calculus II (152) is a prerequisite for CS 206, 314, 336 and 440. One course serves all three programs.

Conflict

CS 206 or Math 477, not both

Credit is not given for both. Use 206 for the CS minor and choose a different math elective, such as 354 or 252.

Overlap

Linear algebra pays off twice

640:250 is required for the math minor and is a prerequisite for CS 323, 334, 428 and 462.

Suggested

Data-focused CS path

120 → 121/122 → 205 → 206 → 336 Data Management → 461 Machine Learning. Pairs well with econ 421 Forecasting and Big Data and 424 Machine Learning for Economics.

Check

Some CS pages are incomplete

345 has no link on the minor page, and 415 Compilers is listed there but is no longer offered. The 425 page is titled Brain-Inspired Computing, though the minor list calls it Computer Methods in Statistics.

Check

Double counting

The math minor page states no overlap rule, and the CS page's overlap sentence does not say which major it means. Confirm with SAS advising.
